How to play
  • New here? Boot Camp (top of this menu) walks you through everything in 3 minutes.
  • Left-click one of your countries to select it. Right-click any other country to send troops. Hover a target first to see the route, the travel time and your odds.
  • Buy units in the side panel. Hold Shift to buy 5. Hotkeys: Q infantry, W tanks, E artillery, R warships, T aircraft, Y missiles.
  • 1–4 sets how much of the army to send (25% to all). G lets you pick a target with left-click.
  • A air strike (aircraft fly 3,500 km, bomb, then return). B artillery bombards a neighbor nonstop. M fires a missile up to 6,000 km.
  • Warships make sea crossings faster and smash enemy landings. Without ships, troops landing from the sea fight at a penalty.
  • Big economies (USA, China, Germany…) pay more and count more toward victory. Grab them!
  • Every nation has a superpower, like Russian winter or island fortresses. Yours is the icon next to your flag.
  • Capitals (★) defend harder. Take an enemy capital to loot half its treasury and split its empire in a civil war. Guard yours!
  • Armies that win battles earn up to 3 veteran stars and fight harder. Fresh recruits dilute them.
  • U opens the Shop: research that applies instantly, up to the Nuclear Program, and ready-made forces delivered to your capital. N fires a nuke, but the whole world will turn on you.
  • Your nations raise free militia (🛡) that guard them. The Autopilot (P) spends part of your income on defenders where attacks are coming, moves idle troops to threatened borders and strikes back at lost nations.
  • Complete contracts (bottom right) for cash. Watch the world news for disasters, revolutions, arms deals and coups.
  • With fog of war on, enemy troops show as ? until your land, armies or aircraft are close.
  • Space pauses. Scroll to zoom, drag to pan. Open Diplomacy to offer peace or alliances. Allies let your armies pass through.
  • Red dashed routes and arrows at the screen edge warn you about incoming attacks. Click an arrow to jump there.
  • Online: the host runs the game. Press Enter to chat. If someone drops out, the AI looks after their empire until they rejoin with the same name.
  • Earn experience and medals in every war to rank up and unlock empire colors and titles. Press H for all controls.
